# A4, A5 and A3: implementation and validation

> Historical r170 stage report. PRs #32–34 were consolidated into #35 with the
> native r186 renderer and its later fixes. See [current implementation and validation](remaining-graphics-progress.md).

2026-09-19. Base: main `94891e1` (including adaptive quality and performance tools).
Renderer remains Three.js r170. The separate r186/TSL migration worktree is untouched.

## Review stack

| Order | Branch | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `codex/graphics-a4-shadows` | Deterministic PCSS and fitted sun frusta |
| 2 | `codex/graphics-a5-lighting` | Hero light envelopes and quarter-resolution shafts |
| 3 | `codex/graphics-a3-particles` | Reproducible atlas, fallback and current-frame depth fades |

The existing committed A-stage implementation was reused from `58ac6c2` and
`90b9d09`, separated into these stages, and integrated with main. Important
integration changes: the sun frustum is fitted after the environment reset;
adaptive resolution is retained; shafts have an explicit tier flag and run in
both composer implementations; the window point system also uses the atlas.

The work is committed on feature branches. It has not been merged into main or
deployed to production. The strict full verification gate is not green; the gaps
below are review requirements, not waived tests.

## Performance

Every HIGH probe in every stage returned **60, 60 FPS**, with no renderer errors.
The following are short ULTRA probes starting at 16 seconds, two governor samples.
They are startup measurements of changing scenes, not steady-state benchmarks.

| Scene | A4 samples | A5 samples | A3 samples | Final A3 resolution scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Independence Day | 33, 37 | 33, 36 | 43, 46 | 0.8 |
| Terminator 2 | 45, 49 | 42, 47 | 56, 52 | 0.9 |
| Day After Tomorrow | 44, 46 | 44, 46 | 42, 48 | 0.8 |
| War of the Worlds | 44, 46 | 41, 44 | 42, 50 | 0.8 |
| Evangelion | 49, 50 | 46, 51 | 60, 55 | 1.0 |

All short probes ended with the ULTRA tier label; reduced drawing-buffer scale
does not change that label. Twelve-second final-build playback probes show what
happens after the governor has more time to respond:

| Scene | Final tier | Final scale | Last three FPS samples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independence Day | HIGH | 1.0 | 60, 60, 60 |
| Terminator 2 | ULTRA | 1.0 | 60, 60, 60 |
| Day After Tomorrow | HIGH | 1.0 | 60, 60, 60 |
| War of the Worlds | ULTRA | 0.8 | 60, 60, 60 |
| Evangelion | ULTRA | 1.0 | 57, 57, 56 |

Thus the strict native-ULTRA 60 FPS target is **not met**. Main's existing governor
trades resolution and then tier for performance; its threshold also allows
Evangelion's 56–57 FPS range without a demotion. That policy was preserved.

### Disabled-feature profiles

Only disposable built bundles were modified; source and production build settings
were not disabled. Each feature was profiled independently at its own stage.

| Scene | A4 PCSS disabled | A5 shafts disabled | A3 atlas/depth fade disabled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independence Day | 60, 60 | 32, 35 | 41, 44 |
| Terminator 2 | 60, 60 | 43, 47 | 56, 49 |
| Day After Tomorrow | 60, 60 | 40, 46 | 56, 52 |
| War of the Worlds | 60, 60 | 41, 44 | 53, 50 |
| Evangelion | 60, 60 | 48, 50 | 60, 60 |

PCSS is a significant cost. Shafts-disabled results are close to enabled results
in these short probes; particle impact varies by scene. Because the governor can
choose different scales, these are diagnostic comparisons, not fixed-resolution
GPU millisecond measurements. The checked-in shadow code retains its tested
16 blocker plus 16 filter samples; an experimental even-index eight-tap subset
was rejected because it under-covered one side of the search disk.

## Before production

- Get a clean full Chromium run, including camera reset, and run WebKit where the
  required browser binary is available.
- Accept the measured adaptive-quality tradeoff or further tune PCSS to meet a
  stricter native-ULTRA frame budget.
- Review and merge A4, then A5, then A3. The separate engine migration is not part
  of this stack.
