# ULTRA tier and 4K assets

Approved direction: Steve asked to upgrade the scene animations to 4K after a review that found every scene is a realtime Three.js render whose resolution is capped by the HIGH tier's 1.7 device-pixel-ratio limit, plus four fixed-resolution assets that would look soft at 4K.

## Scope

1. **ULTRA quality tier** in `dist/cinema.js`: native pixel ratio up to 2, 4096 shadow map, bloom held near HIGH's pixel count. It unlocks only on desktop displays with `devicePixelRatio >= 1.5`; the existing FPS governor still demotes it. The renderer's startup pixel-ratio cap in `dist/simulation.js` rises to 2. `dist/telemetry.js` accepts the new tier name.
2. **Ceiling hint** `data-quality-ceiling` on the canvas so lazily constructed modules can choose asset resolution and tessellation once, at construction.
3. **Assets gated by the ceiling**:
   - `dusk-2k.hdr` (Poly Haven 2K, CC0, MD5 verified) for the visible sky and PMREM when ULTRA is possible; `dusk.hdr` remains for everything else.
   - `nebula-4k.webp` (4096x2048, Real-ESRGAN x4 from the shipped 1536x768 original, seam-padded, Lanczos to 4096) for the space scenes.
   - `explosion-puff-4k.webp` (32 Blender Cycles frames at 512x512, packed 8x4 into 4096x2048) with the atlas cell size parameterised in `dist/baked-explosion.js`.
4. **Tessellation** doubled for planet, sun, black-hole, fireball and tripod silhouettes in `dist/cosmic.js` and `dist/terrestrial.js` when the ceiling is ULTRA.
5. **Tests**: telemetry accepts `ultra`; baked-explosion unit test covers the 4K atlas selection; a Playwright desktop probe at `deviceScaleFactor: 2` asserts the ULTRA tier, pixel ratio 2, the 4K asset requests, and reverse-scrub equality. CI keeps DPR 1 so existing HIGH expectations hold.
6. **Provenance**: `dist/assets/SOURCES.md`, `tools/README.md`, `tools/asset-baseline.json`, and a `docs/ultra-4k.md` note.

## Out of scope

Surface texture maps stay at 1K. No temporal or history-dependent upscaling. No change to phone budgets or to CI's software-WebGL viewport.

## Verification

`npm run check` (unit, certificate, asset checksums, build, Chromium and WebKit suites) plus a local 4K capture of representative scenes for visual review.
